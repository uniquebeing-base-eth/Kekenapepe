    KEKENAPEPE · UYO RUN

    How to play

    1. SteerSwipe left or right anywhere on the road to change lanes.
    2. DriveHold FASTER to boost. Hold BRAKE to slow or stop.
    3. Police checkpointBrake before the checkpoint. Keep at or below 25 km/h as you reach the barrier to get cleared.
    4. Pick up passengersBus stops are on the roadside. Stop nearby and line up to board; stop again at the destination and tap DROP OFF for a ₦150 fare.
    5. Road musicTap the music-note button to open the player; the ride pauses while it is open. Choose ADD FILES and select audio from your device. Added tracks are available for this session.
    6. Daily missionFinish today's distance, coin, or passenger goal for a ₦250 bonus. Complete missions on consecutive days to build a streak; progress and streaks save on this device.
    7. FuelTap 1L · ₦500 or 5L · ₦2,500 any time. Each litre adds 25% fuel.
    8. ContinueAfter a crash, continue for ₦3,000. Each extra continue in that race costs three times more.

    Pick coins, dodge traffic, and enjoy your Uyo cruise. No wahala!
